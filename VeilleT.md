## **1. Context and objectives**

In a fast-changing technological environment, a company’s ability to anticipate change, identify opportunities, and reduce risks is a key factor for competitiveness.
The implementation of a structured technology watch plan makes it possible to:

- improve the quality of technical decisions,
- optimize internal processes,
- reduce costs linked to errors or unsuitable technology choices,
- strengthen innovation and overall performance.

# **2. Technology watch methodology**

## **Leveraging technological knowledge**

**Relevance of the information found**

Technology watch relies on targeted research, driven by the company’s real needs: optimizing internal tools, improving processes, and anticipating technical evolutions.
The information collected must be directly applicable and useful for solving concrete problems.

### **Technical English proficiency**

An effective technology watch requires using English-language sources: official documentation, engineering blogs, specialized forums, and international conferences.
This ensures access to the most recent and most relevant information.

### **Analysis and synthesis**

Each piece of information is analyzed, put into context, and summarized in order to draw actionable conclusions for internal projects.
The goal is to turn raw data into clear recommendations.

## **Setting up a technology watch**

### **Selecting and ensuring relevance of sources**

Selected sources must be reliable, credible, and aligned with the company’s challenges.
Examples: official websites (AWS, Microsoft, PCSoft), technical blogs, knowledge bases, and professional communities.

### **Verification and accuracy of information**

Each piece of information is verified using several criteria: date, consistency, source reputation, and the existence of evidence.
Cross-checking multiple sources helps validate reliability.

### **Collection tools
To strengthen the quality and reliability of the technology watch, two particularly relevant tools can be integrated into the process: Techniques de l’Ingénieur and Inoreader. *Techniques de l’Ingénieur* is a recognized French-language reference source, known for the scientific rigor of its content, its in-depth analyses, and its thematic dossiers written by experts. In addition, Inoreader is a powerful automated monitoring tool that aggregates RSS feeds, filters information, creates smart rules, and organizes watch topics by theme. Thanks to its automation and classification capabilities, Inoreader simplifies the collection, analysis, and distribution of information while ensuring continuous monitoring of official and specialized sources.**

The watch relies on tools that help automate and organize collection:

- Feedly (RSS),
- Google Alerts,
- Daily.dev,
- Notion / Confluence for the knowledge base,
- Trello / Jira for tracking topics.

## **Analysis of collected data**

### **Creating a knowledge base**

Information is categorized by theme, technology, potential impact, and priority.
This knowledge base becomes an internal reference that teams can consult.

### **Generating hypotheses**

The analyzed data makes it possible to formulate hypotheses: adopting a new tool, improving a process, anticipating a migration, or automating a task.

### **Comparison and contrast**

Information from different sources is compared to identify trends, divergences, risks, and opportunities.

### **Synthesis**

Conclusions are presented as summary notes, comparison tables, or operational recommendations.

## **Sharing hypotheses or scenarios**

### **Disseminating information**

Technology watch results are shared through:

- monthly reports,
- knowledge-sharing meetings,
- dashboards,
- a collaborative internal space.

The goal is to make information accessible and actionable.

### **Decision-making support**

Technology watch becomes a decision-support tool that helps:

- guide technical choices,
- prioritize investments,
- anticipate risks,
- strengthen technical consistency.

1. Key KPIs to measure the effectiveness of technology watch

| KPI | Objective |
| --- | --- |
| Information relevance rate | Measure the quality of the watch |
| Average dissemination time | Assess how quickly information is shared |
| Number of technical improvements identified | Track detected opportunities |
| Number of improvements implemented | Measure real impact |
| Number of risks anticipated | Reduce incidents and unexpected costs |
| Savings achieved / costs avoided | Justify the budget |
| Innovation opportunities detected | Contribute to the long-term strategy |
1. Benefits for the company (H1, H2, H3)

| Horizon | Key benefits |
| --- | --- |
| H1 – Short term (0–1 year) | Fast problem resolution, optimizing what exists, reducing incidents |
| H2 – Medium term (1–3 years) | Process improvement, anticipating evolutions, better planning |
| H3 – Long term (3–6 years) | Innovation, competitive advantage, strategic alignment |

# **5. Conclusion**

Implementing a structured technology watch plan is a strategic investment that helps the company gain efficiency, quality, and innovation.
Thanks to a rigorous methodology, reliable sources, in-depth analysis, and clear dissemination, technology watch becomes a true performance lever and a decision-support tool.
